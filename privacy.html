Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026.

Kali Rclone Storage is a personal integration. When the account owner authorizes access, rclone may access Google Drive file contents, names, metadata and storage quota, and may upload, download, modify or delete files only as directed by the operator's configured commands.

Google OAuth credentials and tokens are handled by rclone on the operator's authorized devices. Google Drive files remain in the user's accounts or are transferred to destinations configured by the operator. This static informational website does not receive tokens or Drive contents. No advertising, sale of Google user data, or third-party data sharing is part of this integration.

Access is retained while authorized and may be revoked through Google Account third-party access settings. The operator can remove local rclone remote configuration and tokens, and manages the retention or deletion of transferred files independently.

Questions can be directed to the support email displayed in the Google OAuth consent screen. Hosting providers may process ordinary request logs under their own policies.
